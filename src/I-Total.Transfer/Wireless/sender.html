I-Total Transfer

Send selected photos and videos to the PC running I-Total Transfer. Only the files you choose are sent.

1. Pair with the PC

2. Choose and send

      Some browsers (notably Safari when picking from Photos) may convert HEIC to JPEG before the page sees it; the PC stores exactly the bytes the browser delivers. Each file is hashed on this device and checked again on the PC. An existing file is kept and the incoming one is renamed, never overwritten. This page cannot list your photo library: iCloud-only originals that are not on the phone cannot be sent. Keep this page in the foreground; iOS may suspend it if you switch apps, and a suspended upload is discarded rather than kept as a partial file.
